# Hi, I'm Matyas Broukal 👋

Founder and full-stack developer. I build products end to end: mobile apps, web platforms, backends, data pipelines and the AI features on top of them.

## What I'm building

### 📱 [M](https://mtheapp.com)
A social app for meeting people in real life: dating, friends, events and professional networking, all in one app.
- **iOS & Android** app in Flutter, shipped to both stores with automated fastlane deploys
- **Backend** on Parse Server (Node.js) with cloud jobs that import thousands of events every hour
- **AI profile intake:** users talk about themselves or share links, and Azure OpenAI builds their profile
- **M Reach:** a separate professional-networking mode for founders, investors and people working in startups
- **M Safe:** live-location sharing and SOS for people meeting a date in person

### 🔎 [Brouky](https://brouky.tech)
A global VC and startup intelligence platform. Founders, investors and analysts search and match investors and startups, get AI-powered recommendations, and VCs can claim and manage their profiles.
- **Web app** in Next.js, Tailwind and shadcn/ui
- **Supabase** (PostgreSQL with row-level security, edge functions)
- **Data pipeline** in Python with Playwright scrapers, which gathers and merges data on funds, startups and events worldwide
- **AI matching** with LLMs, plus Stripe subscriptions

### ⚖️ [LegalTree](https://legaltree-visualization.vercel.app)
Legal tech that gives you a contextual understanding of contracts and court decisions. Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT and a multi-step AI workflow maps its arguments, clauses and how they connect into an interactive tree you can explore.
- Next.js and TypeScript, document extraction, LLM analysis pipeline

### 🏦 [Bank of Lithuania Decisions Analysis](https://legal-national-bank-analyst.vercel.app)
A structured analysis of 846 consumer-dispute decisions by the Bank of Lithuania. It supports full-text and semantic search across the decisions, the legal arguments used and the legislation cited.
- Python scrapers and parsers, AI categorisation of every decision, static search website

### 📅 [M Social Calendar](https://v0-social-event-calendar.vercel.app)
A web calendar of social events in Prague and across Czechia, where you can find events and meet people who share your hobbies. It shares its events and accounts with the M app.
- Next.js, Google Maps, Parse (Back4App) auth with Google / Apple sign-in

### 🎉 [Bachelor Party Quest Machine](https://project-edulp.vercel.app)
A party game: spin a random quest, race the clock and upload photo proof (or owe shots). Built for a real bachelor party, with groups, users and live scoring.
- React + Vite frontend, Express + TypeScript API, Supabase, and a 45-check smoke test suite run against production
